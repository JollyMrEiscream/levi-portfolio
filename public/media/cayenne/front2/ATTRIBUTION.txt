Changes made: background removed (rembg isnet-general-use segmentation); converted to
ink line art (difference-of-Gaussians edges, off-white #EEF0F2 on #0B0B0B); reflections
inside the windshield and side glass suppressed, leaving only the glass outline; text on the
front "Cayenne S" display placard blanked, leaving its outline; small stray strokes removed;
headlight LED glow added; animated as a 4 s seamless push-in loop (at most 1.5%); reframed
and cropped. Not mirrored, because the car already faces left. No people. The photo has no
registration plate, only a dealer display placard, and its text was blanked.
